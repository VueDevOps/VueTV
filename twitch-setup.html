Step 1 of 6

Get your account ready

Computer + TV

Use the Twitch account whose followed channels you want on the TV. You don’t need to stream or create a channel broadcast.

  1. Sign in to Twitch on your computer.
  2. Verify your account email and enable two-factor authentication in Twitch’s security settings. Twitch requires both for developer registration.
  3. On the streamer, open Vue TV Preview from your apps. Pressing Home still opens the original Google TV launcher.

Step 3 of 6

Find your public client ID

Computer
  1. Find your app in the console and choose Manage.
  2. Confirm Client Type: Public. Save the change if needed.
  3. Copy the value labelled Client ID.
Client ID is the value the TV needs.

It identifies your application and is public. A public device-code app does not need a client secret. Leave “New Secret” alone.

This field stays in memory only and is cleared when you reload.

This guide does not send the ID to the TV or check it with Twitch. You can also give Codex the public Client ID to help enter it. Keep passwords, tokens, and client secrets out of chat.

Step 4 of 6

Start sign-in on the TV

TV remote

In Vue TV Preview, follow this path:

Connections/Connect Twitch/Twitch client ID
  1. Enter the Client ID from the developer console into the TV field. Check for spaces or missing characters.
  2. Choose Get sign-in code.
  3. Wait for a code to appear. Keep that dialog open on the TV while you complete the next step.
There is no code to copy from this guide.

The TV asks Twitch for a fresh code. Use only the code currently displayed in the TV dialog. If you leave the preview or cancel, start again.

Can’t find the preview in Your apps?

Open Google TV’s full app list and look for Vue TV Preview. The original Google TV home is still the default. If the app is missing, ask Codex to relaunch or reinstall it. You don’t need to change your default launcher.

Step 5 of 6

Authorize on your phone

Phone / computer
  1. Open Twitch’s activation page below. For this Public app, use the public-device activation URL.
  2. Sign in to the account whose followed streamers you want. Enter the code shown on your TV.
  3. Review the app name and permissions, then choose Authorize. The requested scope is user:read:follows.
  4. Keep the TV dialog open. The preview checks for approval automatically; wait several seconds for “Twitch connected”.
Open Twitch activation
Use the public activation link.

The current TV dialog prints the generic activation address. The link here adds ?public=true, as shown in Twitch’s public device-flow documentation. If a valid fresh code is rejected on the generic page, try this link.

Step 6 of 6

Check your live-following row

TV remote
  1. Select the TV icon in the header to open your live follows, or scroll down Home to Live from your follows. In Vue TV 0.5, the profile button at the top left opens Connections; the gear opens the streamer's native quick settings.
  2. Use Connections / Refresh content after sign-in if needed.
  3. If someone you follow is live, select their card. It attempts to open that channel in the official Twitch TV app; if the link is not handled, it opens Twitch instead.

What does this first version support?

Real followed-live channels with thumbnails, category and viewer count. It refreshes about every two minutes while open. To avoid moving focus, it may defer a refresh while you browse a content row; use Connections / Refresh content. It doesn’t integrate chat or replace Twitch’s player.

Will I need to sign in again?

Vue TV renews expired access tokens automatically. If upgrading from 0.20.1 or earlier, reconnect once to save a refresh token. Regular use can keep you signed in beyond 30 days, but Twitch may require authorization after 30 days without a refresh or after access is revoked. Your app registration and Client ID can be reused.

How do I disconnect?

Connections / Disconnect Twitch clears this preview’s saved access and refresh tokens. To revoke the app’s access at Twitch too, remove your registered app under Other Connections in Twitch Connections.
